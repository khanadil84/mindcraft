Laser Dodge

An arcade survival game where the player dodges sweeping laser beams.
Objective: Survive long enough to reach the extraction timer.
Controls: Use Arrow Keys or WASD to move.
Survive: 30.0 seconds
Mechanic: Predict and dodge rotating laser beams.
Win: Survive for 30 seconds.
Lose: Touch a laser beam.
